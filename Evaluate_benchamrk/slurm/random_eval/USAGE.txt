Random-subset evaluation on Killarney
===================================

Sync the repository with git, then run from MKDT, just like random_ssl.sh:

  bash Evaluate_benchamrk/random_eval.sh

This Bash launcher directly submits 40 ordinary jobs from the existing checkout.
The worker uses StdEnv/2023, python/3.11.5, and $HOME/ENV, as random_ssl.sh does.
The fixed tasks.tsv is tracked in git. Python runs only in the GPU workers.

Jobs 01..38 evaluate the other 500 encoders. Job 39 evaluates the twenty
TinyImageNet/ResNet18/1% encoders after jobs 6334332 and 6334333. Job 40 evaluates
the twenty TinyImageNet/ResNet18/2% encoders after jobs 6334334 and 6334335.
Finished checkpoints need no dependency. Missing checkpoints require the named
active Killarney training jobs. Failed parents cancel their dependent evaluation.

Coverage: 540 encoders, 3240 probes, 324 active random table cells; pretraining
sizes 0.2%, 1%, 2%; both SSL methods; ten seeds; 1% and 5% labels. All three
backbones have main-table probes; ResNet18 also has six downstream targets.

Resources per job: 1 GPU, 1 CPU, 6G. Default walltime is 6h for jobs 01..38 and
12h for 39..40. These are allocation limits, not measured runtimes. Override
BENCH_EVAL_TIME / BENCH_EVAL_TAIL_TIME if needed. Accounts: 36 Wang / 4 Lu.
BENCH_VENV, BENCH_DATA_ROOT, BENCH_CHECKPOINT_ROOT and GPU_TYPE work as overrides.
Checkpoint default: Evaluate_benchamrk/random_ssl_checkpoints.
Data default: <MKDT parent>/data. All datasets must already be staged.

Outputs: Evaluate_benchamrk/random_eval_results/
  submitted_TIMESTAMP_PID.tsv   submitted job IDs
  logs/                        Slurm stdout/stderr
  SOURCE/MODEL/random/ptSIZE/SSL/seed_NNN/evaluations/   probe results
  SOURCE/MODEL/random/ptSIZE/SSL/summary.json            ten-seed mean/sample std
  checklist.tsv                all 3240 probe statuses
  table_summary.tsv            table cells, mean/std, ready-to-copy LaTeX
  progress.json                overall completion and invalid-result details

Re-run the same command to resume; completed/live jobs are skipped, completed
probes are verified/reused, and interrupted heads restart from epoch 1. Saved
encoders remain intact. Existing CVPR .tex tables are not overwritten.

Preview without submitting or writing files:
  bash Evaluate_benchamrk/random_eval.sh --dry-run
